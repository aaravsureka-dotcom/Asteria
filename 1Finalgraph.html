Each dot is one SPHEREx measurement: how bright the object is at one wavelength (color) of infrared light. The shaded zones show the six detector arrays that together cover 102 wavelength bands. Dashed lines mark approximate wavelengths where common molecules absorb or emit light. Circled points are automatically detected dips, where the brightness falls clearly below its neighbours; the label names a molecule that absorbs near that wavelength. These are hints, not proof.
